Change is hard for everyone—even when the change is positive or long overdue. Our role is to help move people along on the journey so that when the change happens, people are ready—they know why they are doing it, what to do, how to do it and when to do it.

Since 2007, every engagement has come through a prime contractor or staffing agency, inside ERP, PM and HR programs. Roles are written around a system. That's exactly why the practice now sells the outcome directly: trained isn't ready, and the Systems Integrator (SI) already knows the system.

Clients get a senior lead, with contract associates for production work, a built OCM toolkit, and a fixed fee by phase.
