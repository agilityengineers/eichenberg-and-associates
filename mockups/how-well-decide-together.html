How we’ll decide together

  1. STEP 115 MIN

    First call

    Is there a reason to talk?

    We look at where your go-live stands and what’s at stake.

    You leave with

    A clear yes or no on a longer conversation.

  2. FIT?

  3. STEP 260 MIN

    Discovery

    What are the needs, and whose?

    We hear from the people closest to the go-live, including your sponsor and program lead.

    You leave with

    A shared picture of the risks.

  4. STEP 360 MIN

    Scoping

    What could we do together?

    We agree on the results, approach and investment.

    You leave with

    Agreement on scope and price, and a time to review the proposal.

  5. STEP 460 MIN

    Proposal

    What else do you need before we start?

    I present the proposal to you and your approver. We confirm approvals and procurement.

    You leave with

    The proposal, a decision date and a kickoff plan.

FIT?Decision point: Do we both see a fit? If yes, we schedule the longer conversation. Either of us can stop at any point.
